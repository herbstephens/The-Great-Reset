## Appendix: How The Great Reset Plays Out

### The Core Idea (Plain English)

Think of the global economy like a game of Monopoly that's been running for generations. Some players inherited hotels on Boardwalk. Others started late with nothing. The game has reached a point where most players can't meaningfully participate—they're just paying rent to people who already own everything.

**The Great Reset doesn't change the rules of Monopoly. It clears the board and lets everyone start fresh.**

The date is **January 1, 2030**.

---

### Three Pillars of the Reset

**1. The Land Commons**  
No one made the land—it was here before any of us. Under the Reset, you keep your home and everything you built, but the *land itself* enters a global commons. Landholders pay a small annual fee based on land value. That fee is distributed equally to every verified human on Earth. Not charity—inheritance. The earth belongs to everyone.

**2. Worker Ownership**  
Companies become at least 50% owned by the people who actually work there. Current owners keep 50% of their equity. This isn't punishment—it's alignment. When workers own their companies, businesses make better long-term decisions. Henry Ford understood this: workers need to afford the products they make.

**3. TIME Protocol**  
Every verified human gets a calendar—24 slots per day, recorded on-chain. When you work, you mint TIME tokens. 1 TIME = 1 verified hour of human labor. This creates a floor for human dignity. No hour of work can be worth nothing. Combined with Land Commons distributions, this forms a Universal Basic Income funded not by taxes but by the commons we all share.

---

### Timeline: 2025–2032

**2025–2026: Build the Infrastructure**  
TIME Protocol launches. People begin verifying their identity (via World ID or similar). The claiming registries open: claim your home, claim your work history, establish your calendar. All claims are provisional, accumulating toward the reset date.

**2027–2028: Reach Critical Mass**  
As millions register their claims, political pressure builds. If 500 million people have claimed their homes on-chain, what government tells them no? Campaigns push jurisdictions to pre-commit to recognition. The movement becomes unstoppable through sheer participation.

**2029: Final Preparations**  
Smart contracts are audited, battle-tested, ready. Legal frameworks solidify in early-adopter jurisdictions. The world watches. Media coverage intensifies. The countdown begins.

**January 1, 2030: The Reset**  
Claims become operative. Mortgage debt on primary residences is cancelled—you own your home. Worker ownership kicks in—cap tables restructure. Land Commons fees begin flowing to everyone. The game continues, but with fair starting positions.

**2030–2032: The New Normal**  
The economy doesn't collapse—it accelerates. Billions of newly debt-free homeowners become consumers again. Worker-owned companies invest in long-term growth. The wealthy discover that 50% of a thriving economy is worth far more than 100% of a stagnant one. Businesses need customers who can afford their products.

---

### Why It Works

The Reset doesn't require revolution. It requires coordination.

Blockchain solves the hard problem: *who claimed what, when, verifiably*. Universal identity (proof of personhood) ensures one claim per human. Smart contracts execute automatically—no corrupt intermediaries.

The wealthy aren't enemies to defeat. They're players to recruit. The message: *Congratulations, you won. The game is over. Join us in building the next one—where your customers can actually afford what you're selling.*

This is how the board clears. Same game. Same rules. New starting positions.

**January 1, 2030. Claim your home. Claim your work. Claim your time.**
