# Preface: Three Cities

I spent my high school years in Flint, Michigan, watching a great American city collapse. I now live in Lisbon, Portugal, in a nation that once commanded global empires and now watches its young people emigrate in search of opportunity. And through my co-founder Santiago Siri, I have come to know Buenos Aires—a city that has experienced more economic collapses, currency crises, and system failures than most countries experience in centuries.

Three cities. Three patterns of decline. One lesson.

Buenos Aires, through Santi's eyes and Argentina's endless cycles, taught me that systems can fail suddenly—that the rules everyone believed in can evaporate overnight, that money in the bank can become worthless, that governments can simply take what they want. Argentina has been reset involuntarily, repeatedly, chaotically.

Flint taught me that decline can be slow, invisible until it's irreversible. That a city can be abandoned by the economy that created it, left to poison its own children while the rest of the country looks away. Flint was reset by neglect.

Lisbon taught me that empires end, that former great powers become peripheral, that the children of colonizers can become economic refugees seeking work in their grandparents' former colonies. Portugal is being reset by history.

These cities—and thousands like them worldwide—share a common truth: the current system is not designed to include everyone. When Capital decides a place is no longer profitable, it moves on. The people remain, forgotten.

This book is about a choice. The current game is ending. We can wait for the collapse—the chaos, the violence, the revolutions that history suggests are coming—or we can choose to reset deliberately, with dignity, with recognition for what was built and hope for what comes next.

**January 1, 2030.**

The Great Reset inverts this.

TIME cannot be bought. It can only be earned by existing. One human equals one day equals one TIME. Your political weight comes from participation, not wealth. A billionaire's TIME equals a farmer's TIME.

When you claim your TIME, you're not just receiving an asset. You're reclaiming your seat in the Capitol. The people's house. Occupied by Capital for too long.

We're taking it back.

# Part II: The Game Is Over

Every game of Monopoly ends the same way.

Nobody wants to join a Monopoly game in its final rounds.

*This is not a failure of the system. This is the system working exactly as designed.*

The solution isn't to change the rules. It's to reset the board.

Same game. Same rules. New starting positions for everyone.

## This Has Happened Before

Every successful civilization understood periodic resets:

**Biblical Jubilee** (Leviticus 25) — Every 50 years: debts forgiven, land returned to original families, slaves freed. Not charity. Law.

**Solon's Seisachtheia** (Athens, 594 BCE) — Mass debt cancellation saved Athenian democracy from collapse. The word means "shaking off of burdens."

**Post-WWII Reforms** — Germany, Japan, Taiwan, South Korea all received coordinated economic resets. Land reform. Debt restructuring. Today they're economic miracles.

**Islamic Zakat** — Mandatory wealth redistribution built into the faith. Not optional giving—structural circulation.

The reset is not radical. It is the most conservative position possible—because it conserves the game itself.

The alternative—allowing accumulation to continue until violent collapse—is the truly radical position. France 1789. Russia 1917. China 1949. Every civilization that failed to reset eventually experienced violent redistribution.

**The Great Reset offers a managed, peaceful, predictable alternative.**

# Part V: Open Democracy

## Every claim is a vote.

When you claim your TIME, it doesn't sit idle. It's automatically spread across every political position in your jurisdiction—local, regional, national, global.

By default, your TIME supports the status quo: current incumbents, current policies. The world as it is.

**To change the world, move your stake.**

## The Interface

For every position, you see:
- The incumbent (current office holder)
- All challengers from the last election
- All primary candidates
- Anyone nominated by the people
- Anyone who self-nominates

Slide your stake between candidates. Watch the live results shift. Your voice, counted instantly.

## Nominate Anyone

Traditional democracy has gatekeepers. Parties choose who can run. Ballot access requires money and signatures. Your neighbor can't run without a machine behind them.

TIME Protocol has no gatekeepers.

Any verified resident of a jurisdiction can be nominated for any position in that jurisdiction. You can nominate your neighbor. Your teacher. Yourself. If they accept and people stake on them, they rise in the rankings.

The people find their own leaders.

---

## Quadratic Voting

Your votes are calculated quadratically: the square root of your staked TIME equals your voting power.

| TIME Staked | Votes |
|-------------|-------|
| 1 | 1 |
| 100 | 10 |
| 10,000 | 100 |

This rewards spreading your stake across many issues over concentrating on one. A person who cares about everything has more influence than someone who obsesses over one thing.

---

## The Clout System

Not just how much TIME you have—but how active you are.

**Base weight:** Your TIME stake always counts. Never goes to zero.

**Participation multiplier:** Vote on more issues, earn up to 2× weight.

**Decay:** No votes for 90 days? Multiplier returns to 1×. Your stake still counts, just no bonus.

This means: an engaged citizen with 1,000 TIME beats an apathetic whale with 1,500 TIME.

Engagement is rewarded. Apathy isn't punished—just not rewarded.

Capital can't outspend you. It can only be outvoted by people who show up.

# Part VI: The Tax Revolution

## Human ↔ Human: Tax-Free

Every transaction between verified World ID holders carries zero tax. Send money to your daughter. Split dinner with a friend. Pay your neighbor for babysitting. Tax-free, forever.

This is not a loophole. This is the design.

Humans should be free to transact with each other. The state has no business taxing gifts between family members, tips between friends, or exchanges between neighbors.

---

## Commercial Transactions: Auto-Taxed

When money flows between humans and businesses, tax is calculated automatically based on jurisdiction:

- **Business → Human (income):** Withheld at your jurisdiction's rate
- **Human → Business (purchase):** VAT/sales tax at combined rate
- **Multi-jurisdiction:** 50% buyer's rate, 50% seller's rate

---

## Self-Withholding

You control your taxes. When you receive income from a business, the calculated tax amount is auto-separated into your Tax Hold—but it's your money, under your control.

The protocol tracks what you owe. You decide when to pay.

You can:
- Release funds to your main wallet
- Pay directly to your jurisdiction (if they've opted in)
- Export records for traditional filing

No more accountants computing withholding. No more fear of audits. No more annual reconciliation nightmares. Just clear numbers, under your control.

---

## The Deal for Governments

**Adopt TIME Protocol and receive:**
- Perfect tax collection on all commercial activity
- Real-time revenue flow
- Near-zero evasion (every commercial transaction is logged)
- Elimination of filing bureaucracy

**In exchange:**
- Let humans transact freely with each other
- Accept that peer-to-peer is not commerce
- Register your tax rates in the protocol

Capital gets taxed. Humans don't.

This isn't ideology. It's efficiency. Governments get more revenue with less friction. Citizens get freedom and clarity.

Everyone wins except tax evaders.
